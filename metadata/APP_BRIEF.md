# Guit Recall

## What it is

Guit Recall is a visual-memory workbook built around playing cards. It is for people who want to practise remembering card order, spotting a replaced card, or recognising five-card patterns. This brief quotes the British English interface.

## Launch and onboarding

1. On first launch, the app opens directly to the studio. There is no onboarding sequence. The top row shows “CARD STUDIES” and “Deck”, followed by “Guit Recall”, “A workbook for visual memory”, and a progress bar labelled “0 / 30 STUDIED”.
2. “Start first study” opens “First impressions”. The screen shows “SEQUENCE · 01/10 · FOUNDATION”, the navigation controls “Studio” and “Studies”, and the instruction “Read the five cards from left to right, row by row. Hide the hand when you are ready.” The five numbered cards under “STUDY HAND” are A♠, 3♥, 7♣, 9♦, and K♠, in that order.
3. “Hide cards” replaces the study hand with five empty numbered positions and a card tray. The instruction becomes “Choose the cards in their original order.” Tap five cards to enable “Check order”. The result offers “Next study” and “Practise again”.
4. From the initial studio, the user can also choose “In sequence”, “Spot the change”, “Read the pattern”, “Free practice”, or “Deck”. “Revisit errors (0)” is initially disabled. On later launches, the app restores the last main screen and any saved study state.

## Screens

### Studio

The home screen is headed “Guit Recall”. “CARD STUDIES” appears above it. The subtitle starts as “A workbook for visual memory” and changes to “Every study visited” after all 30 studies have been checked if any need a replay, or “All 30 studies mastered” if none do. The progress label runs from “0 / 30 STUDIED” to “30 / 30 STUDIED”.

- “Deck” opens “The deck”.
- “Start first study” opens “First impressions” on a fresh workbook. “Next unfinished study” opens the first study without a checked answer once progress has been recorded. “Resume · First impressions”, for example, opens the currently active unfinished study at its saved stage. The title after “Resume ·” comes from the active study and can be “Free practice”.
- “In sequence” opens the sequence list. Its tile says “Memorise the cards, then rebuild their order.”
- “Spot the change” opens the change list. Its tile says “Compare two hands and find the card that was replaced.”
- “Read the pattern” opens the pattern list. Its tile says “Identify the strongest pattern in five open cards.”
- Each of the three tiles shows its own progress, from “0 / 10 STUDIES” through “10 / 10 STUDIES”.
- “Revisit errors (0)” shows the number of studies whose latest checked answer needs a replay. When enabled, it opens “Revisit errors”. After all 30 studies have been checked, the studio can also show “Revisit 1 missed studies”, with the count varying, when missed studies remain and the resume button is absent.
- “Free practice” opens the current free-practice hand.

### “Revisit errors”

The top row shows “Studio” and “REVIEW”. The heading is “Revisit errors” and the instruction is “A correct replay clears a study from this list.” The list uses the same tier headings and numbered study titles, with “SEQUENCE”, “CHANGE”, or “PATTERN” above each title. “Studio” returns home. Tap any row to reopen the study. If it is still showing its result, tap “Practise again” to retry it.

### In-sequence study

The heading is the study’s title. Above it is a label such as “SEQUENCE · 01/10 · FOUNDATION”. “Studio” returns home and “Studies” opens the “In sequence” list.

- “STUDY HAND” shows five numbered, face-up cards and the instruction “Read the five cards from left to right, row by row. Hide the hand when you are ready.” Tap “Hide cards” when ready.
- “Choose the cards in their original order.” appears above five numbered slots and a tray containing the original cards and extra cards. Tap a card face to fill the next position. VoiceOver names the card by rank and suit, such as “Ace of Spades”, and identifies unfilled slots as “Empty position 1”, and so on.
- “Undo last card” removes the most recent choice and makes that card available again. “Check order” opens the result once all five slots are filled.
- “STUDY RESULT” reports the score, for example “3 of 5 positions correct”. It lists the correct card for each position, for example “1. Ace of Spades”, and includes “Your pick: Queen of Hearts” where the selected card differs.

### Spot-the-change study

The heading is the study’s title, with a label such as “CHANGE · 01/10 · FOUNDATION”. “Studio” returns home and “Studies” opens the “Spot the change” list.

- “STUDY HAND” shows three to five numbered cards and “Study each position. One card will be replaced.” Tap “Show the second hand” to advance.
- “SECOND HAND” briefly shows card backs, announced as “Covered card” by VoiceOver. When the faces appear, follow “Tap the card that changed in this second hand.” Tap a card face to select its position. A checkmark and outline mark the choice. Tap another card to change the choice. VoiceOver announces both position and card, for example “Position 2, Queen of Clubs”.
- “Check change” opens “STUDY RESULT” once a position is selected. It shows “Correct” or “Try this one again”, the original and replacement cards, and a sentence such as “Position 2 changed from Seven of Hearts to Queen of Clubs.” After a miss it also shows the selected position, for example “You chose position 1.”

### Read-the-pattern study

The heading is the study’s title, with a label such as “PATTERN · 01/10 · FOUNDATION”. “Studio” returns home and “Studies” opens the “Read the pattern” list.

- “OPEN HAND” shows five numbered, face-up cards with “Which pattern best describes these five cards?” Tap one of the three or four named choices. The selection indicator moves when a different answer is chosen.
- The possible choice labels across the workbook are “High card”, “One pair”, “Two pair”, “Three of a kind”, “Straight”, “Flush”, “Full house”, “Four of a kind”, and “Straight flush”.
- “Pattern guide” opens the reference sheet. “Check pattern” opens the result once an answer is selected.
- “STUDY RESULT” shows “Correct” or “Try this one again”, the correct pattern and its rule, and the open hand. A miss also shows the selected answer, for example “Your answer: Two pair”.

### Result navigation for numbered studies

- “Practise again” restarts the same exercise and clears the current answer. Sequence and change return to “STUDY HAND”. Pattern returns to “OPEN HAND”.
- “Next study” opens the next unchecked study later in the same mode, or the first unchecked study anywhere in the workbook when there is no later one in that mode.
- When every study has been checked, “Revisit errors” replaces “Next study” if any studies need practice. Otherwise, “Back to studio” replaces it and returns home. “Studio” and “Studies” remain available from the result.

### “Free practice”

The heading is “Free practice”, with “SEQUENCE / FREE PRACTICE” above it. “Studio” returns home and “Studies” opens the “In sequence” list. The hand uses the sequence flow and the same “Hide cards”, “Undo last card”, and “Check order” controls. Each deal has five cards to remember and a seven-card tray.

The result shows the same per-position review and a running total, for example “1 practice hands · 60% accuracy”. “New hand” deals a fresh hand and returns to “STUDY HAND”. “Practise again” returns to “STUDY HAND” with the same deal. Both preserve accumulated practice statistics.

### “The deck”

“Deck” opens this sheet from the studio. “Monochrome cards” switches the card artwork between colour and monochrome throughout the app. The sheet shows four example aces labelled “Spades”, “Hearts”, “Clubs”, and “Diamonds”, followed by “Every suit has a distinct shape. VoiceOver reads the rank and suit of each card.” The × button is announced as “Close deck” and returns to the studio. The sheet can also be dragged down to dismiss it.

### “Pattern guide”

“Pattern guide” opens this scrollable sheet from an unanswered or unsubmitted pattern study. The × button is announced as “Close” and returns to the exercise with the current selection intact. The sheet can also be dragged down to dismiss it. It lists these rules in this order:

| Pattern | Rule |
|---|---|
| “Straight flush” | “All five ranks run in sequence and all five cards share a suit.” |
| “Four of a kind” | “Four cards share a rank, one from each suit.” |
| “Full house” | “Three cards of one rank sit alongside a pair of another rank.” |
| “Flush” | “All five cards share a suit without forming a run.” |
| “Straight” | “Five ranks form an unbroken run. An ace may sit below two or above the king.” |
| “Three of a kind” | “Three cards share a rank. The other two have different ranks.” |
| “Two pair” | “Two different ranks each occur twice.” |
| “One pair” | “Two cards share one rank. The other three ranks differ.” |
| “High card” | “All five ranks differ. They do not form a run or share a suit.” |

## Features

- “In sequence” has ten fixed studies that ask the user to rebuild a five-card hand from a tray with extra cards.
- “Spot the change” has ten fixed studies in which exactly one card is replaced at the same position between two hands.
- “Read the pattern” has ten fixed studies that ask for the strongest pattern in a five-card hand. “Pattern guide” explains all nine patterns.
- “STUDY RESULT” gives position-by-position corrections for sequence, a before-and-after comparison for change, and the correct pattern and rule for pattern.
- “Revisit errors” collects numbered studies whose latest checked answer was incorrect. A correct replay removes the study.
- “Free practice” supplies random sequence hands and tracks checked hands and cumulative positional accuracy separately from workbook progress.
- “The deck” previews the four suits and offers “Monochrome cards”. VoiceOver identifies rank and suit, and the interface adapts to larger accessibility text sizes.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- “Check order” is disabled until all five positions are filled. An already chosen card cannot be selected again, and the whole tray stops accepting picks once the fifth position is filled. Use “Undo last card” to take back choices one at a time. It is disabled when the answer is empty.
- “Show the second hand” briefly covers the cards. Wait for the faces to appear, then tap the position where the card changed. “Check change” stays disabled until a position is selected.
- “Check pattern” stays disabled until a named pattern is selected. The five card faces are for inspection. Choose one of the answer buttons below them.
- “Revisit errors (0)” is disabled until a numbered study has an incorrect checked result. An incorrect replay puts or keeps that study on the list. To clear it, open the study, tap “Practise again” if the result is showing, and submit a correct answer. Clearing the last item disables the studio control again.
- “STUDIED” counts distinct studies with a checked answer, including misses. Replaying a study does not advance that count. “Every study visited” can therefore appear while the error list still has entries.
- “Next study” can jump to a different mode or to an earlier numbered study if there is no later unchecked study in the current mode. After all 30 have been checked, follow “Revisit errors” or “Back to studio”.
- Opening a study row again can show its previous result. Use “Practise again” to start it over. In “Free practice”, “Practise again” repeats the same hand, while “New hand” deals a different one. Checking either hand adds to the practice statistics.

## Starter content and resume

A fresh workbook contains 30 fixed studies, ten per mode, all available from the start. The first “Free practice” hand is randomly dealt. Study counts and practice statistics start at zero, and there are no prefilled answers or recorded results.

The app saves the last main screen, the stage of each opened study, partial answers, checked results, the current practice hand and statistics, and the “Monochrome cards” setting. Relaunching can return directly to an exercise or its result. The studio’s “Resume ·” button points to the active unfinished exercise. Other opened studies can be resumed from their rows.

## Permissions

None.

## Absent

Login and accounts, in-app purchase, ads, analytics, user-generated content, an account deletion flow, and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are all absent.

## Data and support

Workbook progress, answers, practice statistics, and the card appearance preference stay on the device. There is no on-screen “Contact” or “Support” control.

## Scanning and health

None.

## Platform

Supports iPhone and iPad on iOS or iPadOS 17.0 or later. The build declares portrait, upside-down portrait, and both landscape orientations. Wider layouts can place the study instructions and cards side by side.

The interface follows the selected app language and supports British English, Arabic, and Traditional Chinese. Arabic uses right-to-left reading order for the interface and card positions. The display name is “Guit Recall” in all three languages. There is no region-specific gameplay or content.
